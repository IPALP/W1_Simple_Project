## PAIN TO GAIN

A Unity game about driving a car through a field of obstacles against the clock. Crashing hurts your car, but it also earns you points, so you have to decide how much damage is worth the score.

## How to play
- Move with **WASD** or the **arrow keys**.
- Hit obstacles to gain points, but each one damages your car. Bigger obstacles hurt more and pay more.
- Grab the **2x booster** to double your score.
- Grab **health pickups** to restore health and **clock pickups** to add extra time.
- The game ends when your health or the timer reaches zero.

## Features
**Week 1**
- Player car controller (WASD and arrow keys)
- Random obstacle spawner
- Several obstacle types, each with its own value ( either gain points or lose points )
- 2x score booster
- Timer, score counter and restart screen

**Week 2 update**
- Camera that follows the car's position without rotating, for an isometric-style view
- Health bar, with different damage per obstacle
- Risk/reward scoring: obstacles hurt but give points
- Repair kits and clock pickups
- Game over screen with the final score
- Exhaust smoke particles
- New 3D model (dumpster) made in Blender

## Why I made it
This is my first Unity project. I wanted to learn how the main parts of a game fit together: player control, spawning, UI and collisions. I chose a simple game idea so I could create something playable, then keep improving it.
